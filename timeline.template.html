About this chart

The artwork preserves the original Illustrator layout, colours, portraits and lettering. Appointment details come from the extracted CSV and are approximate years. “Present” means February 2026. Lighter extensions are shown separately and interpreted as later affiliation; the original contains no text legend confirming their meaning. Lettering is preserved as vector outlines for consistent appearance. Names and dates are available as text through search and selection. Yellow and other original colour segments are preserved without assigning new meanings.
